## The scenario

A community meetup that is not about WordPress ("JavaScript Night") wants its own look, not the WordPress one, and keeps no records.

## The steps

1. In the template editor, start from **Basic** and make it yours: dark colors, a logo, a row of elements ([Group elements](../../guides/group-elements/)), a QR code and a signature ([Templates and the editor](../../guides/templates/)).
2. Open **Certificates › Generator** and choose **Switch to Quick**.
3. In **Templates**, mark your template **Use in the Quick generator**; then fill in the event and paste the names (or the Meetup CSV).
4. Download one PDF or a ZIP with one PDF each.

## What it does not do

The Quick generator **saves nothing**: no event, no participants and no record, so the certificates have no code to check. If you want records, use [the Generator with your events](../course-with-classes/).
