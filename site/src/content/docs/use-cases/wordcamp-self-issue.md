---
title: "Self-issue at a WordCamp"
description: "Organizers mark presence by day; each attendee issues their own certificate from the ticket."
---

## The scenario

A WordCamp on CampTix has hundreds of attendees over two days. The organizers do not want to issue anything by hand.

## The steps

1. In **Tickets › Setup › Certificates** set **Attendees get their own** to **When marked as present**, fill the **Event days**, and give each ticket a role.
2. During the event, in **Tickets › Attendees** click the day badge of each person present.
3. After the event the ticket e-mail brings each attendee to the **Certificate** step: they choose their days or table (and talks), declare if the event asks, and **Issue certificate**.
4. A wrong name is fixed with **Correct and reissue**; an organizer can **Revoke** from **Tickets › Certificates**.

## What it does not do

Organizers cannot issue in bulk, and nothing is e-mailed ([WordCamp.org](../../scenarios/wordcamp/)).
