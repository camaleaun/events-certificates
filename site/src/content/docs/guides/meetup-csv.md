title: "Bring a Meetup list"
description: "Get the attendee list from Meetup.com, or any spreadsheet, into an event."
---

## From Meetup.com

1. On the group's page, open the **past event**, then **Attendees**.
2. Open **Manage** (the gear icon) and choose **Attendee details**: Meetup downloads a CSV file. Only the group's organizers see the export.
3. In the event's **Add participants** (or in the Generator) choose **CSV file** and send it.

People who answered **No** are left out, and those Meetup marks as absent come in as **no-shows**. Tick that they all attended if the file says nothing about attendance.

The Generator draws this step by step as pictures in your language. They show the WordPress NYC group as a sample (the largest WordPress meetup in the world), and Curitiba in Brazilian Portuguese; the pictures are files of the plugin and nothing is requested from Meetup.

## From a spreadsheet

Save it as **CSV**, with a column named **Name** and, if you have it, **Email**. Or copy the rows and paste them in the participants box.

## Typed lines

One person per line, with the e-mail and the role if you have them: `Ana Lima, ana@example.com, Speaker / Organizer`. A person with two roles gets two certificates ([Roles and languages](../roles-and-languages/)).
