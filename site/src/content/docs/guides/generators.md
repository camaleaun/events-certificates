## Generator

**Certificates › Generator** works from your events. Pick an event; the list shows its participants, and clicking a name previews the real certificate of that person. The header has three buttons:

- **Download current**: the person on the screen.
- **Download PDF**: everyone who attended, in one PDF.
- **Download ZIP**: one PDF each.

Each attendee also has a **PDF** button on their row. The bar at the top opens the command palette (also <kbd>⌘K</kbd>) with the same actions.

## Quick generator

The **Quick** generator (the **Switch to Quick** button at the top left of the Generator; **Switch to Records** brings you back) needs no records and draws its own template, **Meetup** (the **Meetup** template that comes with the plugin; **Templates** says which template it uses and lets you pick another with *Use in the Quick generator*). You fill in the event, the signer and a list of names (paste, type or drop a CSV) and download. The preview is drawn in your browser and the PDFs are made on download; nothing is saved on the site. A single download makes up to 400 certificates.

The same generator is also a **page for visitors** (a block you can put on a page), for communities that want organizers to make their own certificates; see [Block and shortcodes](../../reference/block-and-shortcodes/).
