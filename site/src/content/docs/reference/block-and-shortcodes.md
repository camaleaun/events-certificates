---
title: "Block and shortcodes"
description: "Put the generator and the certificate pages in your pages."
---

- **Quick generator** (block): the generator for visitors, on any page.
- **Get my certificate** (block): self-service by e-mail.
- **Check a certificate** (block): the form to type a code.
- **Tutorial step picture** (block and shortcode): the pictures of the steps to get the attendee list from Meetup.

The blocks are in the inserter under **Events Certificates**. The check page can also be a page of your site: choose it in Settings › Verification, and its address becomes the certificates' address.
