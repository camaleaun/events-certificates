---
title: "Both together"
description: "What changes when GatherPress and CampTix are active on the same site."
---

When **GatherPress and CampTix** are active together, the plugin has its **own top-level Certificates menu** and its own **Settings** screen (neither plugin hosts it).

- From **CampTix**: attendees issue their own certificate from the ticket, with days and the declaration ([WordCamp.org](../wordcamp/)).
- From **GatherPress**: the **Certificates** action on an event's row imports and syncs RSVPs and check-ins ([GatherPress](../gatherpress/)).
- The same person gets one certificate per event, whichever way they came in.
- Like in the other two, lists cannot be exported or imported, and there is no Generator or Quick generator.

This is mostly how the plugin is developed and tested; most sites use one or the other.
