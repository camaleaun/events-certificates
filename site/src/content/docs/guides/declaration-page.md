title: "Declaration page"
description: "An extra page the attendee fills in when they issue: the days and the talks they attended."
---

The **declaration page** is a second page of the attendee's certificate step, where they **declare** what they attended: the days and, with the *wc-post-types* schedule, the **talks** they sat in. The certificate then says exactly that.

It belongs to **CampTix** tickets: it is written when the attendee issues their certificate from the ticket ([WordCamp.org](../../scenarios/wordcamp/)), and only for the attendee role. It is **off** until you turn it on in **Settings**, under **Features**; the **Declaration** tab then holds its options.

## What the attendee does

1. Opens the **Certificate** step of their ticket.
2. Chooses the days (or the contributor-day table) and the talks they attended.
3. Fills in anything the event asks for and uses **Issue certificate**.

To fix something later they use **Correct and reissue**: the first certificate is marked replaced.

## In the template

The editor has a **Declaration** tab for the second page of the certificate. Without the declaration page switched on, that tab is the only part that changes.
