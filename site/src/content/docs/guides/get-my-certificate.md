---
title: "Get my certificate"
description: "Let participants get their own certificate with their e-mail."
---

With **self-service** on, a participant can ask for their certificate on a page of your site: they type the e-mail they registered with and receive a link to it. Only people marked as attended get one, and the link works only for that e-mail.

You choose in Settings whether the e-mail is confirmed by a link (**simple** or with a sign-in link) and, for CampTix, which tickets count.

The page is the block **Get my certificate** (see [Block and shortcodes](../../reference/block-and-shortcodes/)).
