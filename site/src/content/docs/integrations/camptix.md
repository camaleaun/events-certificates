---
title: "CampTix"
description: "Use the attendees of CampTix."
---

With CampTix active, certificates can be made from its **attendees**: the Certificates screen sits under Tickets, an attendee's page links to their certificate, and the ticket e-mail can carry it. Self-service looks the attendee up by the e-mail they used to buy. The declaration page (the days attended, talks) is for CampTix tickets.
