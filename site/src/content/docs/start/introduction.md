Events Certificates makes attendance certificates for the events of a WordPress community: a meetup, a WordCamp, a workshop.

You add an **event**, bring its **participants**, mark who **attended**, and each of them gets a **PDF certificate** with their name, the event, the date and a code. Anyone can check the code on your site.

## What you need

- WordPress 7.1 or later and PHP 7.4 or later.
- Nothing else: the plugin works on its own. If you sell tickets with **CampTix** or run events with **GatherPress**, it works with them too (see [CampTix](../../integrations/camptix/) and [GatherPress](../../integrations/gatherpress/)).

## Where things are

After activating the plugin you get a **Certificates** menu:

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Certificates | The certificates issued: view, download, correct, revoke or send to the Trash. |
| Events | Your events and their participants. |
| Generator | Pick an event and download the certificates of who attended. |
| Quick generator | Paste names, fill in the event, download: nothing is saved. |
| Settings | Who signs, the language, whether certificates can be checked. |

Templates (the look of a certificate) are opened with the **Templates** button on the Certificates screen.

## Try it without installing

The [Playground link](https://playground.wordpress.net/?blueprint-url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/camaleaun/events-certificates/trunk/.wordpress-org/blueprints/blueprint.json) opens a WordPress in your browser with the plugin and a made-up event.
